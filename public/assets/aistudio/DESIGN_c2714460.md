## Brand & Style

### Personality & Emotional Resonance
The design system embodies the soulful, vibrant, and warm cultural spirit of Southern Vietnam (Nam Bộ). Rooted in the living traditions of Đờn ca tài tử, Vọng cổ, Múa bóng rỗi, and Hát bội, the interface evokes nostalgia, regional pride, warmth, and contemporary celebration. The voice is affectionate and hospitable—just like the phrase "Mình ơi"—welcoming youth and heritage enthusiasts alike into an interactive cultural journey.

### Design Style: Folk Modernism & Tactile Richness
A harmonic fusion of Vietnamese traditional decorative arts and clean modern layout principles:
- **Palette Vitality**: High-saturation celebratory tones balanced on soft, warm parchment surfaces (`#FAF8F2`).
- **Visual Texture**: Delicate paper grain, subtle theatrical gold leaf accents, and rhythmic organic swoops reminiscent of khăn rằn check patterns, bamboo leaves, and river currents.
- **Editorial Fluidity**: Expressive, flowing typographic accents balanced by authoritative classical serifs and razor-sharp modern sans-serif body text.

## Colors

The palette is derived directly from the ceremonial vestments, stage costumes, and pastoral landscapes of Southern Vietnam:

- **Hồng Sen (Primary - `#E84A91`, accent `#F0549C`)**: Representing the romantic emotion of Vọng cổ, lotus blossoms, and feminine grace. Used for primary CTAs, active highlights, key thematic badges, and hero gradients.
- **Hoàng Yến / Vàng Tươi (Secondary - `#F5BC25`, tint `#FAD044`)**: Reflecting ceremonial brass, the golden ritual trays of Múa bóng rỗi, and vibrant southern sunlight. Used for celebratory accents, badges, interactive discovery tokens, and secondary action highlights.
- **Xanh Lá Tre / Cốm (Tertiary - `#8CC63F`, deep `#6DB33F`)**: Reflecting the miệt vườn riverbanks, bamboo groves, and youthful freshness of Đờn ca tài tử. Used for status indicators, nature/geography tags, and contrasting action buttons.
- **Nâu Đất Son / Đồng Cổ (Neutral Base & Dark Accent - `#5C4D12`, deep `#3D330B`)**: Representing ancient theatrical masks, carved wooden instruments, and ancestral soil in Hát bội. Serves as deep grounding text, rich theatrical cards, and dark container surfaces.
- **Nền Sáng Kem / Lụa Tơ Tằm (Background / Canvas - `#FAF8F2`, surface `#FFFFFF`)**: A warm, breathable silk-cream canvas that eliminates harsh clinical white and provides high-contrast legibility for bright folk colors.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy bridges traditional southern ornamentation and contemporary legibility:

- **Display & Calligraphic Accents**: Special masthead logotypes and feature titles utilize custom calligraphic lettering inspired by southern signage, folk verse, and hand-lettered opera banners. In standard layouts, `EB Garamond` in Italic/Bold functions as the core classical serif headline, capturing the historic gravitas of Southern theater.
- **Body & Interface**: `Plus Jakarta Sans` provides warm, highly legible, modern geometric humanist forms. Its open counters and friendly tone ensure comfortable reading across long cultural articles, mini-game cards, and metadata.
- **Quotes & Excerpts**: Rendered in `EB Garamond` italic with quotation ornaments to preserve the poetic intimacy of vọng cổ verses and folk sayings.

## Layout & Spacing

### Grid Structure
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column fluid layout with `1.25rem` (20px) margins and `1rem` (16px) gutters. Single-column card stacks and horizontal snap-scroll rails for card galleries.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px)**: 8-column layout with `2rem` (32px) margins and `1.25rem` (20px) gutters. 2-column card arrays for regional heritage sites.
- **Desktop (> 1024px)**: 12-column layout constrained to `1200px` max-width with auto centering, `2.5rem` outer margins, and `1.5rem` gutters.

### Rhythm & Vertical Flow
Content sections breathe through rhythmic alternating background panels: transitioning between silk-cream canvas zones and vivid thematic full-bleed containers (such as the festive lotus pink community banner or the imperial bronze theater block).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses tactile folkloric layering and warm ambient glow rather than standard stark shadows:

- **Level 0 (Base Canvas)**: Soft silk paper tone (`#FAF8F2`) devoid of harshness.
- **Level 1 (Card & Content Blocks)**: Raised white or tinted card surfaces with ultra-subtle warm borders (`1px solid rgba(92, 77, 18, 0.08)`) and ambient diffused dropshadow: `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(92, 77, 18, 0.08)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Thematic Cards & Tarot Decks)**: Stacked collectible aesthetic. Elevated cards carry a multi-layered shadow: `box-shadow: 0 10px 25px -4px rgba(232, 74, 145, 0.15), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(92, 77, 18, 0.06)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Dialogs & Floating Cultural Drawers)**: Deep dramatic elevation with tinted ambient wash: `box-shadow: 0 20px 40px -8px rgba(92, 77, 18, 0.25)`.
- **Theatrical Light Accents**: Critical calligraphic titles and badge highlights use a soft golden radial luminescence (`box-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(245, 188, 37, 0.4)`).

## Shapes

The design system maintains a balanced, friendly curvaceous geometry (`roundedness: 2`, base `8px / 0.5rem`):

- **Standard Elements & Cards**: `rounded-md` (8px) to `rounded-lg` (16px) for cards, preview images, and input surfaces, creating a modern yet approachable container style.
- **Thematic Collectible Cards (Bộ Tứ Thẻ Bài)**: `rounded-xl` (24px) corners, mirroring physical collectible playing cards and ceremonial amulets.
- **Buttons & Pills**: Fully rounded pill shapes (`border-radius: 9999px`) for action buttons, filter tags, and interactive blind-box trigger buttons ("Xé túi mù ngay").
- **Ornamental Frames**: Top corners of cultural heritage cards may feature subtle asymmetric arches or scalloped silhouettes inspired by southern temple architecture and wooden communal house gables.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA ("Xé túi mù ngay")**: Full pill radius (`rounded-full`), solid background in vibrant lotus pink (`#E84A91`), bold white uppercase typography (`Plus Jakarta Sans 14px 700`), subtle hover lift with glowing shadow (`0 8px 16px rgba(232, 74, 145, 0.3)`).
- **Secondary CTA ("Khám phá Miss Văn hóa")**: Solid bamboo green (`#8CC63F`) with crisp white text, or golden yến (`#F5BC25`) with deep bronze text (`#3D330B`).
- **Ghost & Heritage Buttons**: Warm cream background with a `1.5px` border in `#5C4D12` and gold/bronze hover transitions.

### Cultural Collectible Cards (Thẻ Bài Di Sản)
- Dedicated 4-pillar cultural card suite representing *Tri Âm* (Đờn ca tài tử - Green), *Tình Quân* (Vọng cổ - Pink), *Nguyện Cầu* (Múa bóng rỗi - Gold), and *Nghĩa Khí* (Hát bội - Bronze/Earthy Ochre).
- Each card possesses a colored header block, clean portrait/illustration container with `12px` inner border radius, custom calligraphic title, and bottom quote block framed with traditional quotation marks.

### Metric & Counter Stat Tiles
- Rounded rectangular containers (`16px` border-radius) in vibrant base colors (Gold `#FAD044`, Lotus `#F0549C`, Green `#8CC63F`).
- Prominent oversized numerals (`36px` to `48px`, font-weight 800) accompanied by minimalist icon badges and concise label text in deep bronze.

### Heritage Location & Profile Cards
- Horizontal or alternating 2-column layout: crisp photography on one side paired with structured editorial storytelling on the other.
- Titles rendered in `EB Garamond` with highlight keywords styled in deep bronze or primary pink bold weights.

### Interactive "Blind Box" / Mystery Reveal Module
- Gamified collectible interface with tactile textured overlays, shimmering golden contours, and high-impact visual celebratory modals designed for youth engagement on mobile web.
